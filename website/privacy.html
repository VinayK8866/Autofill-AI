Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

1. Overview

Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. Filli AI is designed with a privacy-first architecture, ensuring that your confidential personal information and private API keys remain under your control.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign in using Google, we store your email address and profile name via Supabase Authentication to manage your subscription tier and cloud quota.
  • Local Storage: Your persona profiles, custom field mappings, and domain memories are stored locally in your browser's encrypted extension storage.
  • Payment Details: We do NOT collect or store your payment card details. All financial transactions and payment processing are handled securely by our Merchant of Record, Paddle.com.

3. How We Use Information

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Provide, maintain, and authenticate your cloud form filling services.
  • Enforce monthly fill limits and verify your Pro subscription status.
  • Respond to your customer support inquiries.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated cloud records at any time by contacting our support team at vinaykondabattula@gmail.com.

5. Third-Party Services

We work with trusted third-party providers:

  • Paddle.com: Merchant of Record and payment processing.
  • Supabase: Authentication and user plan management.
  • Google Cloud: Gemini AI inference for cloud form generation.
