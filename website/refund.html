We want you to be completely satisfied with Filli AI. If you purchase a Filli AI Unlimited Pro subscription and are not satisfied with the product for any reason, you are entitled to a full refund within 14 days of your initial purchase date.

2. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund within your 14-day evaluation period, please contact us:

  • By sending an email to vinaykondabattula@gmail.com with your registered account email and order reference.

3. Subscription Cancellation

You can cancel your recurring monthly subscription at any time with zero hassle:

When you cancel, your account will remain active with full Unlimited Pro features until the end of your current paid billing period. You will never be charged again after cancellation.
